My first review since joining, six months in. Format is annual, 360-degree
(manager + peer input).

**Strengths.** Ramped up faster than expected given the remote/async
onboarding — I was already contributing to the activity-data pipeline in my
first month. Classic ML fundamentals are strong, especially around
recommendation systems. Peers consistently mentioned I'm responsive and
easy to collaborate with async, which matters a lot given the Brazil/US time
overlap is only a few hours a day.

**Growth areas.**

1. *Production ML / MLOps.* Most of my modeling work to date has stayed
   in notebooks. I've started shipping (the workout-streak model, behind a
   feature flag) but haven't yet closed the loop with monitoring/alerting.
   Alex wants to see that gap closed, and for shipping-and-monitoring to
   become my default mode rather than the exception.
2. *Visibility in cross-team/leadership settings.* This is explicitly not
   about English proficiency — it's about speaking up with confidence in a
   second language, remote, cross-culture setting. I've made real progress
   here since February (from staying silent, to prepping points ahead of
   time, to speaking up unprompted with concrete numbers). The ask now is
   consistency, not a one-off good meeting.
3. *End-to-end ownership.* I've contributed pieces to several projects
   but, until recently, hadn't owned one fully — scoping, building,
   shipping, and being the point of contact. The workout-streak project is
   a good first attempt; Alex wants to see it carried through to a full
   stakeholder-facing delivery.

Overall trend is positive and each growth area already has early momentum
going into this review — these are being turned into IDP actions
([idp-2026-001](../idp/2026.md), [idp-2026-002](../idp/2026.md),
[idp-2026-003](../idp/2026.md)) as of today.
